# Public environment & infrastructure roadmap

Canonical public route: `/roadmaps/environment`. Overview: `/environment`. Distinct from the project roadmap.

**Source of truth:** `src/config/environment.ts`. This is a public-safe planning and verification view, not the actual private asset inventory.

The six phases are:
1. Inventory and foundation.
2. Networking and security.
3. Compute and virtualization.
4. Storage and backup.
5. AI and automation.
6. Reliability and expansion.

Allowed milestone statuses: `planned`, `verify`, `in-progress`, `complete`. `complete` must be backed by private evidence and approval before publication. Costs may remain `To be scoped`; never turn a rough figure into a confirmed budget.

Safety constraints: preserve normal Xfinity household networking, segregate proposed lab infrastructure through an approved gradual plan, prove backups and recovery first, and keep private addresses, management consoles and credentials off the public site. Nothing on these pages operates hardware.
